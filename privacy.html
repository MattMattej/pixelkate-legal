Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data in the Pixel Kate private automation application.

1. Data Collection

As an internal automation tool, this application does NOT collect, store, or process personal data from the general public. It does not use trackers, cookies, or analytics on public users.

2. Third-Party Integrations

The application connects to third-party APIs (such as TikTok, fal.ai, and OpenRouter) solely for the purpose of generating and publishing our own content to our official accounts. We comply with the respective Developer Terms of Service of these platforms.

3. Security

Access to the application is restricted to authorized internal personnel only. API keys and credentials are securely stored using Google Cloud Secret Manager.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
